# 🚀 Publishing Guide — Super Easy Version

Hi! This guide gets your website live on the internet for **free**, and shows
you how to get your **own real name** (like `ravenscraftvisuals.com`) too.

**Quick truth first:** Putting your website online is 100% free, forever.
But owning a real ".com" name is something NOBODY can give away for free —
every website owner in the world pays a small yearly fee for their name
(usually **$10–15 a year**, about the cost of one pizza 🍕). After that, we
use free tools for everything else!

We'll go slow, one click at a time. Ready? Let's go! 🚀

---

## 🧸 Part 1: Put Your Website Online (100% Free)

We'll use a free helper called **Netlify**. It's like a magic mailbox — you
drop your website files in, and it gives you a real web address instantly.

1. Go to **netlify.com**
2. Click **Sign up** (you can use just an email, or your Google account)
3. Once you're in, look for a big box that says something like
   **"Drag and drop your site output folder here"**
4. Get your website's `dist` folder — this is a folder with all your
   website's files already built and ready to go (ask me and I can make sure
   it's ready, or your project's Publish/Export option can give it to you)
5. **Drag that whole `dist` folder** onto the box on the Netlify page
6. Wait about 10–20 seconds ⏳
7. 🎉 Netlify gives you a free web address, like:
   `https://cheerful-panda-123456.netlify.app`

That's it — your website is LIVE! Anyone in the world can visit that link
right now.

---

## ✏️ Part 2: Make the Free Address Look Nicer

The random name Netlify gives you isn't very pretty. Let's fix that part for
free:

1. In Netlify, click on your new site
2. Click **Site configuration** (or **Site settings**)
3. Find the button that says **Change site name** (or **Edit site name**)
4. Type something like `ravenscraft-visuals`
5. Click **Save**

Now your free address looks like:
`https://ravenscraft-visuals.netlify.app` 🎉

This part is still 100% free — but it's a Netlify address, not one you truly
"own" like a real dot-com name.

---

## 💎 Part 3: Get Your OWN Real Name (Small Yearly Cost)

This is the part that costs a little money — but it's cheap, and once you
buy it, **it's yours**. Nobody can take it away as long as you keep paying
the small yearly fee (like renewing a library card).

### Step 1: Buy your name

1. Go to a "domain registrar" website — good, trustworthy ones are:
   - **namecheap.com**
   - **porkbun.com**
   - **google domains / Squarespace Domains**
2. Type the name you want in the search box, like `ravenscraftvisuals.com`
3. If it's available, it'll show you a price (usually **$10–15 per year**)
4. Click **Buy** / **Add to cart** and check out
5. 🎉 Congratulations — you now OWN that name!

### Step 2: Point your name at your free website

Now we tell your new name where to find your website.

1. Go back to **Netlify** → your site → **Domain management** (or
   **Domain settings**)
2. Click **Add a domain** or **Add custom domain**
3. Type the name you just bought, like `ravenscraftvisuals.com`
4. Netlify will show you some settings (they look like a few lines of text
   called "DNS records") — don't worry, just follow the on-screen
   instructions; Netlify tells you exactly what to copy
5. Go to the website where you bought your name (Namecheap, Porkbun, etc.),
   find **DNS Settings**, and paste in what Netlify showed you
6. Wait a little while (sometimes minutes, sometimes up to a day) for the
   internet to "learn" about the change

🎉 Once that's done, typing `ravenscraftvisuals.com` in any browser, on any
device, anywhere in the world, shows YOUR website — and you truly own that
name.

## 🆘 If something goes wrong

- **Site looks blank after uploading?** Make sure you dragged the whole
  `dist` folder, not just one file inside it.
- **Domain not working yet?** DNS changes can take a few hours (sometimes up
  to 24-48 hours) — this is normal, just wait and try again.
- **Not sure which name to buy?** Try a few ideas at Namecheap's search bar —
  it'll tell you instantly if a name is taken or available.
- **Still stuck?** Tell me exactly what you see on the screen and I'll help
  you figure out the next click!
