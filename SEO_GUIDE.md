# 📈 SEO Guide — What Was Built & What Actually Makes You "Popular"

## What I just added to your website's code

1. **Better page title & description** — shows up in Google search results and
   browser tabs. Written to include real search terms people use: "SE
   Wisconsin photographer," "wedding photographer," "quinceañera photographer,"
   etc.
2. **Keyword meta tag** — a supporting signal (Google barely uses this
   anymore, but other search engines and tools still reference it).
3. **Social share previews (Open Graph + Twitter Card tags)** — when someone
   pastes your link into Facebook, Instagram bio, iMessage, Slack, or
   Twitter/X, it now shows a nice preview card with your photo, title, and
   description instead of a bare link.
4. **Structured data (JSON-LD)** — a hidden block of code that tells Google
   "this is a photography business, founded 2005, serving Wisconsin, here are
   the services offered." This is what helps Google show rich results (like a
   business card with your info directly in search results).
5. **`sitemap.xml`** — a map of your page's sections that helps search engines
   discover and understand your content faster.
6. **`robots.txt`** — tells search engines they're welcome to crawl your whole
   site, and politely asks them to skip your private `/admin` page.
7. **Admin page protection** — your private admin panel now tells search
   engines "don't index this" automatically, so it won't accidentally show up
   in search results.
8. **Proper heading structure** — one clear `<h1>` (your main headline), with
   `<h2>`/`<h3>` used correctly for section and card titles. This helps search
   engines understand what your page is actually about.
9. **Descriptive image alt text** — portfolio and logo images already include
   readable descriptions, which helps with Google Image search.
10. **A web app manifest & icon** — small polish that helps your site look
    legitimate and "real" to browsers and search engines.

**⚠️ One thing you must do:** several of these tags use the placeholder web
address `https://www.ravenscraftvisuals.com/`. Once you actually buy your
domain name, search my code for that placeholder (in `index.html`,
`public/robots.txt`, and `public/sitemap.xml`) and swap in your real address
— just tell me your final domain and I'll update it for you in one shot.

## 🎯 The honest part: code is maybe 20% of the job

I want to be straight with you: **good code helps Google understand and trust
your site, but it does NOT make you "popular."** No amount of code can fake
what actually drives real search rankings and traffic:

### 1. Google Business Profile (biggest lever for a local business like yours)
This is **completely free** and usually matters more than anything else for
local searches like "photographer near me" or "Wisconsin wedding
photographer."
- Go to **google.com/business**
- Claim/create your free listing for Ravenscraft Visuals
- Add your service area, phone, hours, photos, and a link to your new website
- Ask happy clients to leave you reviews — this is huge for ranking

### 2. Real reviews
Ask every client to leave a review on Google and Facebook. A handful of
genuine 5-star reviews does more for local search ranking than months of
code tweaks.

### 3. Backlinks (other sites linking to you)
Get listed on:
- The Knot / WeddingWire (for wedding photographers)
- Local Wisconsin business directories / chamber of commerce
- Instagram bio link, Facebook page "Website" field
- Any local blogs, venues, or vendors you've worked with — ask them to link
  to you in exchange for linking back

### 4. Fresh content
Search engines favor sites that update regularly. Every time you upload new
portfolio photos through your Admin panel, that's a small positive signal.
Consider also occasionally writing a short blog-style post (even just a
"Recent Wedding at [Venue]" post) if you want to go further — that's a
bigger project we can discuss separately.

### 5. Page speed & mobile-friendliness
Already handled — your site is a single fast-loading file, fully responsive,
and works great on phones. ✅ Nothing more needed here.

### 6. Time
SEO is slow by nature. A brand-new domain typically takes **weeks to a few
months** to start appearing reliably in search results, even doing
everything right. This is normal — don't be discouraged if you don't see
results in the first week.
